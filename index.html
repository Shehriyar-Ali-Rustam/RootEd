The loop

Four steps. The fourth is the one nobody else builds.

A free video can explain a chapter. It cannot notice that you have misunderstood it. RootEd closes that gap on the same screen.

01

Learn

A 5–10 minute lesson on one chapter of your actual board syllabus. Full transcript, so it works on a slow connection.

02

Practise

Three to five past-paper style questions. Every wrong answer is explained — the specific slip, not a generic hint.

03

Ask

Still not clicking? Tap once. We show tutors who teach that exact chapter — with a 60-second demo so you hear them first.

04

Book

Your parent approves the slot and the price before anything is paid. Session happens. You come back to the chapter and finish it.

Straight answers

Questions we would ask too.

Is RootEd live yet?
No. We are forming the first cohort of 20–30 learners and vetting the first tutors. Joining the list puts you in that group and costs nothing. We would rather say this plainly than put a fake launch date on a page.
Which board and subject are you starting with?
We have deliberately not decided. We are picking the first board and subject from what students actually tell us they are stuck on — the form below asks. Starting with one and doing it properly beats covering nine badly.
Are these certified teachers?
No, and that is the point. They are university students who sat these board papers recently. We verify identity and qualifications, review a demo lesson, and train them on lesson structure and safeguarding before they take a booking. What you get is someone closer to your experience, at a price a Matric family can actually pay. We will never describe them as certified schoolteachers, because they are not.
What does it cost?
Sample lessons are free. Live sessions are set by each tutor — the examples above sit around PKR 800–900 an hour, and RootEd takes a disclosed percentage of that. Final pricing comes out of the pilot, and we will not publish a number we have not tested with real families.
Will it work on a slow connection or a shared phone?
That is a requirement, not a nice-to-have. Every lesson has a full text transcript so it can be read without video, video quality is adjustable, and the whole thing is built for a 360-pixel screen first. Many of the students we are building for share one phone in the household.
How do I know a session actually happened?
Every booking has a state you can see: requested, guardian-approved, paid, completed. A missed session by either side has a defined remedy written down before you book — not decided afterwards over WhatsApp.
